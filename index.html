  • Maximize Clicks with a max per click: commercial CAD 15, generic CAD 11. Search only, no Display or partners. AI Max and Google-written text off.
  • 31 areas: Metro Vancouver, Fraser Valley, Sea-to-Sky, Vancouver Island. People located there only. Every day 6am to 10pm.

How the keywords were chosen

  • 179 phrases checked from Riarh's services, competitor ads and Google's suggestions. Volumes from two sources: Google Keyword Planner for BC, and DataForSEO for Canada. Across 13 terms in both, BC = 14.9% of Canada; city searches match within about 20%.
  • Boundary rule: commercial buyers only. No homes, no healthcare (that is Medico), no tenders or subcontractor job hunting, and tenant improvements dropped (Sandy, high-ticket focus).
  • 206 keywords, exact and phrase match only. No broad match, so Google cannot wander into unrelated searches.
  • Two campaigns: commercial searches (about 900 a month in the target areas) get the higher bid; "general contractor" and "construction company" searches (about 5,200 a month) share the CAD 50/day (commercial 32.50, generic 17.50), with ads that say "Commercial Spaces Only".
